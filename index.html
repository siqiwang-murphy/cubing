Insert one white corner

Keep the white cross on the bottom, with each edge color matched to its side center. Turn the top layer until the corner’s two non-white colors match the two centers beside its slot. If the slot is at the back, rotate the whole cube to bring it to the front before inserting. Use Righty for a right-side insertion or Lefty for a left-side insertion.
